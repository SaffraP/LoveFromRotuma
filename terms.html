Terms & Conditions

These are general business terms rather than legal advice. Last updated: [Date Placeholder].

Deposits (Custom Orders)

A non-refundable 25% deposit, to cover the cost of materials, is required via M-PAiSA before production begins on any custom order.

Premade Dress Payment

Premade (ready-made) dresses are paid in full at pickup, via M-PAiSA or cash.

Custom Orders

Because every custom dress is handmade specifically for the customer, changes requested after production has begun may incur additional charges.

Fabric Availability

Fabric availability changes regularly. If your selected fabric is unavailable, we will contact you to discuss suitable alternatives before production begins.

Production Time

Production is typically completed within one week but may vary during busy periods.

Pickup & Delivery

Pickup is available from our Wailoku shop, or for free at a Suva town meetup spot (Wailoku Bus Stand or McDonald's). For delivery outside Suva — including Nadi and the outer islands — please contact us directly to arrange details and any applicable fees. Delivery dates are estimates and may occasionally change due to weather, road conditions, or other unforeseen circumstances.

Abandoned Orders

Orders will be considered abandoned after one month from the agreed pickup or delivery date unless other arrangements have been made with us in advance.

Returns

Dresses are custom-made and cannot be returned unless faulty. If a garment has a manufacturing fault, please contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order. Plant returns (once available) are accepted only if the plant arrived damaged.

Friends & Family Discount

Friends and family receive a 5% discount. Due to current operating costs, additional discounts are not available.

Pricing

All prices are listed in Fijian Dollars (FJD) and may change without notice.

Alterations & Fittings

Alterations and custom fittings are available in person at our Wailoku shop. Most custom fittings can be completed on the spot.
